Porting a chart to kro by hand is guesswork

A Helm chart is a template program: a value can end up in one field, in five, inside a string, behind a condition, or nowhere. Rewriting that as a kro ResourceGraphDefinition means deciding, value by value, what is safe to expose and how. chart2kro makes those decisions by experiment, checks each one with kro's own code, and tells you why it decided.
